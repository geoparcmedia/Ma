## Caption (copy all)

Bike tour or trek in Morocco? Here are 5 ways to explore the Atlas Mountains and the Sahara with local guides 👇

🚵 Caravan Road – 8-day gravel bike tour, 281 km on old caravan tracks
🏜️ Atlas to Sahara – cycling tour to the Chegaga dunes, gravel, MTB or e-bike
🥾 Hike & Bike – 2 days hiking in the Happy Valley, 3 days riding to the Ouzoud waterfalls
⛰️ Toubkal trek – climb North Africa's highest peak (4,167 m) in 3 days
🐪 Sahara desert trek – walk with camels, sleep under the stars

All tours start in Marrakech, with small groups or private trips, a 4x4 support vehicle on bike tours, and mules on treks.
