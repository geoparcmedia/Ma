Moroccan Odyssey – 15-Day Grand Tour

Experience the imperial cities, Atlas Mountains, Sahara Desert, coastal Essaouira, and more in this unforgettable 15-day journey through Morocco.

Itinerary

Day by day

1Day 1Arrival in Casablanca · Rabat or Tangier

Welcome to Morocco! Today is the start of a great adventure that you will never forget. You can start your tour in either Casablanca or Tangier. On arrival, you will be picked up by our driver and taken to your hotel to relax. If you have time, our professional and friendly driver can help you discover the city with a professional local guide.

2Day 2Chefchaouen, the Blue Pearl

Your tour in Morocco begins with a jump start today. You’ll be picked up by our professional driver and head up to Chefchaouen. This city is a visual delight in blue; walls, doors, windows; stairways, alleyways, and archways. In the afternoon you will be free to enjoy the corners of the city and discover the hidden beauty.

3Day 3Explore Chefchaouen

It is time for more adventure and discovery, it is now time to see and learn more about the Blue Town. Meander the mystical medina, which, despite the number of intriguing shops, is still mainly residential, and where we find several shops selling tourist gifts & gadgets. Your driver is always available if you would like to make a visit to the stunning Rif Mountains.

4Day 4Volubilis · Moulay Idriss · Fes

It is time to leave Chefchaouen and head south to Volubilis, the most extensive remains of a Roman city in Morocco, and one of the most distant of the Empire’s domain. We’ll move on to Moulay Idriss, which, from a distance, looks like a sitting camel. The oldest town in Morocco was founded by Moulay Idriss I in 789. Our journey today will lead us to one of Morocco’s four imperial cities, Fez, where you can have some rest and prepare for tomorrow’s tour around this historical city.

5Day 5Explore the city of Fes

In order to make your stay in Morocco rewarding and rich, we will start our day by a visit to the UNESCO protected Fez Medina and see it through a local’s eyes, with a private guide who was born in Fez. Your guide will help you navigate the narrow and maze-like ancient streets of the medinas, starting in Fes El Bali. Your tour lasts from 9:30 am starting from the King place until 5:30 pm ends in the tanneries, then meet your driver and back to your accommodation to relax after a long day of walking inside the oldest Medina in Morocco.

6Day 6Ifrane · Ziz Valley · Erfoud

Today we will leave Fez early in the morning and head into the south of Morocco crossing the Atlas Mountains. You’ll pass by Ifrane before continuing ahead to Midelt then Ziz Valley. In this beautiful oasis valley, you can have a short walk through the date palms and farms. After a night in the hotel, we’ll be fully rested and ready for the next day’s adventures.

7Day 7Sahara Desert Merzouga (Erg Chebbi)

It is finally time to the discovery of Moroccan Sahara. Today we’ll go to the dunes of Erg Chebbi and enjoy the Sahara Desert. We’ll explore Erfoud and Rissani before preparing for a beautiful night under the stars. Prepare for a camel ride through the dunes, arriving at camp just before sunset. Climb up the nearest sand dune to watch the colorful display as the sun sets behind the colossal dunes. Camp for dinner in the open air and an evening by the campfire enjoying traditional Berber music from the locals under a blanket of stars. Spend the night in a luxury camp.

8Days 8 & 9Tinejdad · Skoura (Valley of Kasbahs)

Rise with the dawn to watch the sunrise behind the sand dunes, a stunning view you should not miss, then we’ll return to the hotel for shower and breakfast. First stop is Tinejdad where lunch is available. Stay in the Skoura palm grove, known as the Valley of Kasbahs. On the second day in Skoura, enjoy a free day to relax in this garden oasis and soak in the calm desert atmosphere.

10Day 10Ouarzazate · Ait Ben Haddou · Telouet · Marrakech

Leave Skoura for Ouarzazate, visit Ait Ben Haddou (UNESCO World Heritage), then Telouet, former palace of the Glaoui family. Continue to Marrakech and explore Jemaa El-Fnaa Square before dinner in your riad.

11Day 11Marrakech guided city tour

Explore Marrakech with a professional guide. Visit Souks including Souk El Attarine, Souk Haddadine, Souk Smata, and the dyers’ souk. See the Koutoubia Mosque, Ben Youssef Madrasa, Marrakech Museum, Saadian Tombs, El Badi Palace, Bahia Palace, and the Jewish quarter.

12Day 12Marrakech · Essaouira

Drive from Marrakech to ancient Essaouira, formerly Mogador, 118 miles (191 km). Explore the 18th-century town, walk along the beach, and enjoy local ambiance with optional activities.

13Day 13Essaouira

On Essaouira’s beach, you’ll find kitesurfing schools. Explore the medina with its craftsmen, little shops, and hidden gems. Enjoy a local fish dish for dinner.

14Day 14Essaouira · El Jadida

Visit El Jadida’s Portuguese City, mostly untouched by tourism. Explore before heading to the new town for dining.

15Day 15Departure from Casablanca airport

End of your Moroccan Odyssey Tour. Our team ensures you will arrive at Casablanca airport 2-3 hours before your flight. Memories to cherish!
