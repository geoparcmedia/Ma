    We are Amasir Voyage

    Morocco, the way we'd show a friend

    We're a small team of drivers and guides based in Marrakech. A lot of our trips start the same way: we meet you at arrivals, load the bags, and on the drive into town we talk about what you actually want to see.

    Some guests want the big loop over the Atlas to the Sahara. Others have one free day and just want to get out of the medina for a few hours. Either way the trip is private, planned around you, with sensible driving days and stops wherever there's something worth stopping for.

    — the Amasir team
