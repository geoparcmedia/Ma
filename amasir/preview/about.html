About us

A small Marrakech team, not a call centre

Drivers and guides who live here, planning private trips we'd be happy to take our own families on.

Our story, in short

Trips built around people, not packages

Amasir Voyage is a small team based in Marrakech. We run private day trips, multi-day tours and airport transfers across Morocco.

Our regular day trips go to Agafay, the Ourika Valley, Imlil, Essaouira and the Ouzoud waterfalls. For longer trips we head over the Atlas to Merzouga and the Sahara, or north to Chefchaouen and Fes. If you have your own idea, we're happy to build the trip around it.

We keep every trip private, plan realistic driving days, and leave a bit of room in the schedule for the things you only discover once you're here.
