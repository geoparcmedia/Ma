# We Gravel Morocco – off-site SEO kit

Everything here is ready to copy and paste. These are the steps that move a new site from page 2 to page 1: Google needs to see that other places trust the site.

## 1. Google Business Profile (do this first)

Create it at https://business.google.com with the email you check every day.

- **Business name:** We Gravel Morocco
- **Main category:** Tour operator
- **Extra categories:** Bicycle tour agency, Hiking tour agency (pick the closest ones Google offers)
- **Service area:** Marrakech, Azilal, Ouarzazate, Zagora (no shop address needed if you do not receive clients at an office)
- **Phone:** +212 660 435 569
- **Website:** https://wegravelmorocco.com/
- **Description (750 characters max):**

> We Gravel Morocco is a local tour operator based in Marrakech and Azilal. Our licensed guides lead small-group and private gravel bike, mountain bike, e-bike and trekking tours across Morocco: the High Atlas, the Happy Valley (Ait Bouguemez), the Draa Valley and the Sahara Desert. Every bike tour has a 4x4 support vehicle, and every trek has mules and muleteers to carry the luggage. Climb Toubkal or M'Goun, ride the old caravan roads to the Sahara, or ask us for a tailor-made tour on your own dates. All tours start and finish in Marrakech, with airport transfers included. We reply within 24 hours.

- **Photos:** at least 10 real photos (riders on the trails, the team, the 4x4, camps). Add 2–3 new photos every month.
- **Products / services:** add each tour with its price "from" and a link to its page.

## 2. Reviews (every client, every tour)

Send this on WhatsApp the day after the tour ends, with the two review links:

> Hi [name], thank you for riding with us! We hope you loved Morocco as much as we loved having you. If you have two minutes, a short review would help us a lot – it is how other travellers find small local teams like ours:
> Google: [your Google review link]
> Tripadvisor: [your Tripadvisor review link]
> Shukran bzaf, and see you on the trails again! – Marouane & the We Gravel Morocco team

Goal: 3 to 5 new reviews a month. Answer every review, good or bad.

## 3. Links from other websites (backlinks)

Each listing below gives the site a link and brings bookings on its own.

**Tour marketplaces** (list each tour, with a link to the matching page on wegravelmorocco.com where allowed):
- TourRadar
- BookAtrekking
- GetYourGuide
- Viator (Tripadvisor Experiences)

**Cycling and outdoor platforms** (publish the routes, add the website in the profile and route description):
- Komoot – one "collection" per tour (Caravan Road, Atlas to Sahara, Happy Valley)
- Strava – club "We Gravel Morocco", routes and the guides' rides
- Wikiloc and AllTrails – Toubkal and M'Goun treks

**Partners** (ask each one for a link to your site on their "activities" or "excursions" page):
- Riads and hotels in Marrakech where your clients sleep
- Guesthouses (gîtes) in the Ait Bouguemez and Imlil
- Desert camps in Chegaga / M'Hamid
- Bike rental and bike shops you work with

**Press and blogs:** cycling clubs and travel bloggers in Europe. Offer a free ride day or photos in exchange for an article with a link (use the email below).

## 4. Social profiles

Make sure Instagram, Facebook and Tripadvisor all have the same name, phone and the link https://wegravelmorocco.com/. Post the link to each new guide on Instagram and Facebook when it is published.

## 5. Google Search Console (once a month)

- Sitemap submitted: https://wegravelmorocco.com/sitemap_index.xml
- Use "URL inspection" → "Request indexing" for each new or changed page (Cycling page, the 4 new guides, the 4 tours with new URLs).
