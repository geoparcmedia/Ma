We Gravel Morocco

Gravel Biking Adventures Across Morocco

We Gravel Morocco is a local team of certified cycling and mountain guides based in Marrakech and Azilal. We design guided gravel bike, mountain bike and e-bike tours on quiet dirt roads and ancient caravan routes – from the High Atlas and the Happy Valley to the Draa oasis and the Sahara Desert – with full vehicle support, comfortable accommodation and delicious local food.

Explore remote landscapes through unforgettable gravel bike tours in Morocco. Every ride becomes an authentic gravel biking adventure guided by experienced local professionals.
