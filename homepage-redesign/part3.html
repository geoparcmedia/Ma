Why Morocco Is Perfect for Gravel Bike Holidays

Morocco offers rich and exciting cycling tours for riders of all levels. Routes connect famous cities such as Marrakesh, Fes, Meknes and Rabat, and cyclists move from desert roads to mountain paths – so every day brings a new experience. For this reason, Morocco is ideal for adventure cycling and off-road travel.

Culture – Gravel Cycling Through Moroccan Heritage

Morocco’s culture is a unique blend of Berber, Arab, and French influences, built over many centuries. As you ride your gravel bike through bustling medinas, peaceful villages, ancient kasbahs, and remote desert tracks, you’ll experience the warmth and hospitality Moroccans are known for. From mosques and old schools to carpets, ceramics, and traditional perfumes, all these elements add meaning to your journey.

Our local guides, deeply knowledgeable about Moroccan history and cycling routes, enrich each gravel bike tour with meaningful stories and cultural insights – so every gravel biking adventure in Morocco becomes a true human experience, not just a ride.

When to ride

Climate for Gravel Biking

Morocco enjoys a diverse climate ideal for gravel bike tours and adventure travel year-round. From mild coastal weather to cooler Atlas Mountain temperatures and warm desert conditions, each region offers unique riding experiences.

Cycle through mountain passes, green valleys, and desert tracks in comfortable and safe conditions. Most of our cycling tours run from March to November, Sahara treks from September to May, and high-mountain treks like M’Goun from June to October.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time for a cycling tour in Morocco?

Most of our gravel and mountain bike tours run from March to November. Sahara Desert treks are best from September to May, and high-altitude treks such as M’Goun from June to October.

Do I need to bring my own bike?

No. You can bring your own bike or rent a mountain bike, gravel bike or e-bike locally through our company.

How big are the groups?

Our groups range from 2 to a maximum of 16 travelers. Private and tailor-made tours for solo travelers, couples, families and friends are also available on request.

What is included in the tour price?

Licensed local guides, accommodation (hotels, riads, guesthouses or camps), the meals listed in the itinerary, mineral water, transport including airport transfers and, on bike tours, a daily 4x4 support vehicle. Flights, visas, tips and travel insurance are not included.

How fit do I need to be?

We offer tours from easy to challenging. Each tour page shows its grade, daily distances and elevation so you can choose the right level – and we can adapt a private tour to your pace.

Where do the tours start and end?

All tours start and end in Marrakech, with airport transfers from Marrakech Menara Airport included.
