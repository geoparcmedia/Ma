The tour

Tour overview

This 8-day gravel bike tour starts and ends in Marrakech. You ride along the Atlas range and over the volcanic Jebel Saghro, following the old caravan road through quiet gorges, kasbahs and valleys until you reach the Draa Valley, Morocco's longest oasis.

It's a proper back-country route: five days of riding on dirt roads, with nights in kasbah hotels and family-run guesthouses.

Day by day

Itinerary

Day1Welcome to Marrakech

We pick you up at Marrakech Menara Airport and drive you to the hotel. Later you meet your guide for a short briefing. With any time left, walk around the old town, Bahia Palace and Jemaa el-Fna square.

Drive 20 minutesAccommodation HotelLevel Easy
Day2Marrakech to Skoura Oasis35 km

We drive south over the High Atlas to the village of Ghassat, where the riding begins. The track runs over red earth and along the river to the Skoura oasis. It's an easy first day to find your rhythm. Night in a kasbah hotel.

Cycling 35 kmElevation +480 m / -490 mDrive 5 hoursAccommodation Kasbah hotelMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day3Skoura Oasis to the Dades Gorges68 km

A short drive takes us to Souk El Had. From there we ride across the Azaghar plateau, nomad country where some Berber families still live in caves. After lunch we continue through the Kati gorges and the Rose Valley to the Dades Gorges. Night in a guesthouse.

Cycling 68 kmElevation +800 m / -490 mDrive 45 minutesAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day4Dades Gorges and Jebel Saghro68 km

We ride from the guesthouse toward the remote Berber villages of Jebel Saghro. A short climb to Boumalne Dades is followed by 15 flat kilometres. After tea with a local family, we cross the rocky Saghro massif to our guesthouse.

Cycling 68 kmElevation +1200 m / -1000 mAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day5Jebel Saghro to the Draa Valley64 km

The day starts with a 5 km climb to the high Saghro plateau and wide views back to the High Atlas. After 15 km of rolling terrain, we drop down the old caravan route into the Draa Valley. Night in a kasbah hotel.

Cycling 64 kmElevation +850 m / -650 mDrive 30 minutesAccommodation Kasbah hotelMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day6Draa Valley and the Tizgui waterfall46 km

An easy day, mostly flat or downhill, through the oasis and past old mud-brick villages. We stop for a picnic in the palm groves before reaching the Tizgui waterfall, then transfer to Ouarzazate for the night.

Cycling 46 kmElevation +400 m / -290 mDrive 1 hourAccommodation HotelMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day7Ouarzazate to Marrakech via Ait Ben Haddou1 hour

After breakfast we follow the caravan road through a green oasis to Ait Ben Haddou, famous as a film location. After lunch there's time for a walk through the old kasbah village, then we drive back to Marrakech.

Walking 1 hourDrive 5 hoursAccommodation HotelMeals Breakfast, lunchLevel Easy
Day8Departure

Transfer to Menara Airport for your flight. Check-out is at 11:30. We hope to see you on another gravel ride soon – ma'a salama!

Drive 20 minutesMeals Breakfast
