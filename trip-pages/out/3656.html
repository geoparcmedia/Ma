The tour

Tour overview

The Ait Bouguemez, better known as the Happy Valley, is a green valley about 30 km long in the central High Atlas, around 210 km east of Marrakech. Berber villages sit between the fields, and above them rises M'Goun, at over 4,070 m the second-highest mountain in Morocco.

This is a gentle trek. You walk four to five hours a day from village to village and sleep in family-run guesthouses, while mules carry the bags. You also visit the Ouzoud waterfalls and the natural bridge of Imi n'Ifri on the way.

Day by day

Itinerary

Day1Arrival in Marrakech

Welcome to Marrakech, the Red City. Look for the We Gravel Morocco sign at Menara Airport, and we'll drive you to your hotel. Explore the city if you like, but be back for the welcome meeting, where your guide goes through the trip.

Accommodation Hotel / riadLevel Easy
Day2Marrakech to the Happy Valley via the Ouzoud waterfalls1 hour

After breakfast we drive about five and a half hours to the valley, passing through Azilal and stopping at the Ouzoud waterfalls. In the Happy Valley we meet the muleteers and walk for an hour to our guesthouse.

Walking 1 hourDrive 5.5 hoursAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day3To Ait Imi and the Sidi Moussa granary4.5 to 5 hours

A 4–5 hour walk through the heart of the valley. We climb up to Sidi Moussa, an old hilltop granary where families kept their grain in small rooms. In the afternoon we walk up the valley from village to village to our guesthouse in Ait Imi.

Walking 4.5 to 5 hoursAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day4Ait Imi to Zaouit Oulmzi5 hours

We walk through the village of Ibaqalliwn to see dinosaur footprints left in the rock millions of years ago. Then we continue to Adazen for a great view of the valley. After lunch we go down to Zaouit Oulmzi for the night.

Walking 5 hoursAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day5Zaouit Oulmzi to Ikhf n'Ighir4 to 5 hours

We follow the wide, green Ait Hakim valley through a string of traditional villages. Lunch is in the shade of big walnut trees, then we walk on to Ikhf n'Ighir for dinner and the night.

Walking 4 to 5 hoursAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day6Ikhf n'Ighir to Agouti4 to 4.5 hours

Our last day of walking crosses the villages of Ait Ziri and Aguerd n'Ouzrou and the Tamzrit plateau, with wide views over the whole valley. Along the way you'll meet farmers in the fields and women washing clothes in the river, and see how the valley's people live from the land.

Walking 4 to 4.5 hoursAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day7Agouti to Marrakech via Imi n'Ifri

After breakfast we drive about five hours back to Marrakech, stopping at the natural bridge of Imi n'Ifri. Night in a hotel or riad.

Drive 5 hoursAccommodation Hotel / riadMeals Breakfast, lunchLevel Easy
Day8Departure

Transfer to the airport for your flight home.
