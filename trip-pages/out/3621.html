The tour

Tour overview

Four days walking in the Sahara with a camel caravan, far from roads and towns. From M'Hamid El Ghizlane we follow dry riverbeds and cross big dunes. At night we sleep in nomad tents under more stars than you have ever seen.

The walking is easy, about five hours a day, and the camels carry the bags. You can also ride a camel for a while if your feet need a rest. On the way there and back you cross the High Atlas and visit the Draa oasis and Ait Ben Haddou.

Day by day

Itinerary

Day1Arrival in Marrakech

Welcome to Marrakech, the Red City. Look for the We Gravel Morocco sign at Menara Airport, and we'll drive you to your hotel. Explore the city if you like before the welcome meeting, where you meet your guide and go through the trip.

Drive 20 minutesAccommodation HotelLevel Easy
Day2Marrakech to M'Hamid and the Draa oasis45 minutes

A long but beautiful drive today. We cross the High Atlas over the Tizi n'Tichka pass (2,260 m), the highest road pass in Morocco. We stop for lunch in the big palm groves of the Draa Valley, then continue to Ras Nkhal on the edge of the Sahara. First night in camp.

Walking 45 minutesDrive 9 hoursAccommodation CampMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day3Oulad Driss to Sidi Naji5 hours

The trek begins. Once the camels are loaded, we walk through golden dunes to the big dune of Erg Mado and climb it for a wide view over the desert. Lunch is a picnic in the shade of tamarisk trees. Night in camp.

Walking 5 hoursAccommodation CampMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day4Sidi Naji to Erg Zahar5 hours

We follow the camels across stony plateaus and climb the dune of Erg Zahar for another great view. Night in camp.

Walking 5 hoursAccommodation CampMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day5Erg Zahar to Erg Smar5 hours

Get up early for the sunrise and have breakfast in the open air. Then we walk deeper into the desert along the dry bed of the Draa River. You can ride a camel for a while, and the camel men will show you how they bake bread in the hot sand. Night in camp.

Walking 5 hoursAccommodation CampMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day6Erg Smar to Oulad Driss, transfer to Agdz

After breakfast we walk for an hour and a half to Oulad Driss and say goodbye to the caravan team. Then we drive to the Agdz oasis and spend the night in a kasbah.

Drive 5 hoursAccommodation KasbahMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day7Agdz to Marrakech via Ait Ben Haddou1 hour

We follow the old caravan road through a green oasis to Ait Ben Haddou, the ksar used in many films. After lunch we walk through the old village, then drive on to Marrakech.

Walking 1 hourDrive 6 hoursAccommodation HotelMeals BreakfastLevel Easy
Day8Departure

Transfer to the airport for your flight home.

Drive 20 minutesMeals Breakfast
