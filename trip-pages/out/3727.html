The tour

Tour overview

This tour starts and ends in Marrakech and packs a lot into eight days. First you trek for three days through the Azaden and Imnan valleys to the top of Toubkal (4,167 m), the highest peak in North Africa. Then you swap boots for a bike and ride two days through the Ouirgane valley and out into the stony Agafay desert.

The trek is challenging and the cycling is moderate. Mules carry the bags in the mountains, and a 4x4 supports you on the bike days.

Day by day

Itinerary

Day1Arrival in Marrakech

We meet you at Marrakech Menara Airport and drive you to your hotel, 15 minutes away. Later you meet your guide for a briefing. If you have time, visit the old town, Bahia Palace and Jemaa el-Fna square.

Drive 15 minutesAccommodation HotelLevel Easy
Day2Marrakech to the Tamsoult valley5 hours

After breakfast we drive about two hours to Imlil, where the trek starts. We meet the muleteers and walk for five hours over the Tizi Mzik pass (2,489 m / 8,166 ft), then down to our camp in the Tamsoult valley.

Walking 5 hoursElevation +790 m / -300 mDrive 2 hoursAccommodation CampMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day3Tamsoult to the Toubkal refuge6.5 hours

A steady climb on an old mule path brings us to the Aguelzim pass (3,550 m), with a superb view of the Toubkal massif. After a picnic we go down to the refuge (3,207 m) and camp for the night.

Walking 6.5 hoursElevation +1100 m / -300 mAccommodation Wild campMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day4Toubkal summit (4,167 m) and Aremd9 hours

Summit day. We start early and reach the top of Toubkal in about three and a half hours. On a clear day you can see for miles in every direction. After some time on the summit we go back down to the refuge for lunch, then continue to Aremd for a night in a guesthouse.

Walking 9 hoursElevation +960 m / -2267 mAccommodation Guesthouse, Dar Aremd or similarMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate to hard
Day5Aremd to Ouirgane via the Imnan valley50 km

Now the bikes come out. We roll down to Imlil, then climb to the Tizi n'Tamatert pass (2,380 m) through the Berber villages of Ikiss and Tinghrin. Lunch is in the Imnan valley, with its terraced fields. After lunch a long, fun descent takes us to Asni, and a short transfer brings us to our guesthouse in the Ouirgane valley.

Note: If you want more riding, you can cycle the extra 18 km to the guesthouse.

Cycling 50 kmElevation +800 m / -1400 mAccommodation Guesthouse or aubergeMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day6Ouirgane and the Agafay desert45 km

A short climb to the Tizi Ouadou pass (1,276 m) warms the legs, and we enjoy the view with a glass of mint tea. Then we ride down the valley to the Lalla Takerkoust lake for lunch by the water. In the afternoon we ride the last section into the Agafay desert and spend the night in a comfortable desert camp.

Cycling 45 kmElevation +750 m / -680 mAccommodation Luxury campMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day7Agafay to Marrakech

After breakfast we drive back to Marrakech for a city tour with a local guide: Bahia Palace, the Koutoubia mosque and the lanes of the old medina, ending at Jemaa el-Fna square.

Accommodation HotelMeals BreakfastLevel Easy
Day8Departure

Transfer to Menara Airport for your flight. Check-out is at 11:30. We hope to see you again soon – ma'a salama!
