The tour

Tour overview

Eight days, six of them on the bike, from Marrakech over the High Atlas to the edge of the Sahara. You ride past Ait Ben Haddou, down through the palm groves of the Draa Valley, across dry lakes and out to the Chegaga dunes, where you spend a night under a sky full of stars.

The riding is mostly on quiet back roads and desert tracks, so it suits a mountain bike, a gravel bike or an e-bike. A 4x4 support vehicle stays with the group every day, so you can take a break in the car whenever you need one.

Day by day

Itinerary

Day1Arrival in Marrakech

We meet you at Marrakech Menara Airport and drive you to your hotel, 15 minutes away. In the evening your guide runs through the tour with the group. If you have time, wander through the old town, Bahia Palace and Jemaa el-Fna square.

Drive 15 minutesAccommodation Hotel or riadMeals DinnerLevel Easy
Day2Marrakech to Ouarzazate45 km

We leave the city and climb into the Atlas over the Tizi n'Tichka pass (2,260 m). The land turns dry and red as we reach Ait Ben Haddou, the old ksar you may know from Lawrence of Arabia and Gladiator. After lunch we get on the bikes and ride through small villages to Ouarzazate, the 'city of cinema'.

Cycling 45 kmDrive 3.5 hoursAccommodation Kasbah hotelMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day3Ouarzazate and the Draa Valley50 km

A one-hour drive takes us to Tizi n'Tinifift (1,800 m), with a view over the volcanic Jebel Saghro and the Draa Valley, Morocco's longest valley at about 1,200 km. From there it's mostly downhill to the Tizgui waterfall and the first oasis. We ride through Berber villages and stop for mint tea with a local family.

Cycling 50 kmDrive 1 hourAccommodation Kasbah hotelMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day4Mezguita to Zagora55 km

Today we follow the Draa oasis, where desert sand meets tall green palms. The river road gives some of the best views in southern Morocco. After 55 km on the bike, the 4x4 takes us the last 50 km to Zagora, the traditional gateway to the Sahara.

Cycling 55 kmDrive 1 hourAccommodation Kasbah hotelMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day5Zagora to Erg Lihoudi55 km

Desert day. A short 4x4 drive takes us to Tamegroute and its Koranic library, which dates back to the 11th century. From there we ride into the Sahara on a varied route over dry lakes and past small green oases, all the way to our camp in the dunes of Erg Lihoudi. Dinner and the night are under the stars.

Cycling 55 kmAccommodation CampMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day6Erg Lihoudi to the Chegaga dunes and Foum Zguid65 km

We drive 50 km across the dunes to Lake Iriqi, a dry salt lake where nomads still pass with their camels. Then we ride along the flat lakebed toward Foum Zguid, where we sleep in a kasbah hotel.

Cycling 65 kmDrive 1.5 hoursAccommodation Kasbah hotelMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day7Foum Zguid to Taznakht and Marrakech30 km

The last ride is an easy 30 km through quiet oases and Berber villages. From Taznakht we drive back to Marrakech for the night.

Cycling 30 kmDrive 6 hoursAccommodation Hotel or riadMeals Breakfast, lunchLevel Moderate
Day8Departure

Transfer to Menara Airport for your flight. Check-out is at 11:30. We hope to ride with you again – ma'a salama!
