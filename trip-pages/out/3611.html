The tour

Tour overview

This is the classic Toubkal trek, and the quickest way to stand on the roof of North Africa. In three days you walk from Imlil up through Berber villages and a rocky high valley to the summit of Toubkal (4,167 m), then back down again.

It's hard work but not technical. Mules carry the bags, and you sleep one night at the base camp and one in a village guesthouse. If you're short on time but want a real mountain, this is the trip for you.

Day by day

Itinerary

Day1Marrakech to Imlil (1,750 m) and the Toubkal base camp (3,207 m)5 hours

We pick you up from your hotel or riad in Marrakech and drive about an hour and a half to Imlil. There you meet the muleteers and start a five-hour walk to the base camp. We pass the village of Aremd (1,950 m) and stop for lunch at the shrine of Sidi Chamharouch (2,360 m), then zigzag up to the base camp. You sleep in the refuge, or in a tent if you prefer, from May to the end of October.

Walking 5 hoursElevation +1457 mDrive 1.5 hoursAccommodation CampingMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate to hard
Day2Toubkal summit (4,167 m) and Aremd (1,900 m)9 hours

We start early for the summit of Toubkal, the highest point in the Atlas and in North Africa. From the top, the views stretch across the Atlas toward the Sahara. We go back down to the refuge for lunch, then continue to Aremd for the night.

Walking 9 hoursElevation +960 m / -2217 mAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate to hard
Day3Aremd to Marrakech45 minutes

After breakfast at the guesthouse we walk down to Imlil and drive about an hour and a half back to Marrakech, arriving around midday. Add a guided tour of the medina if you'd like to see more of the city.

Walking 45 minutesElevation -150 mDrive 1.5 hoursMeals BreakfastLevel Easy
