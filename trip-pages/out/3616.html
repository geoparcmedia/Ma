The tour

Tour overview

Toubkal (4,167 m) is the highest mountain in Morocco and in North Africa. This 6-day trip takes the quieter route to the top, over the Tizi Mzik and Aguelzim passes, through Toubkal National Park and past Berber villages and high valleys.

Before and after the trek you have time in Marrakech, with a guided tour of its palaces, gardens, souks and Jemaa el-Fna square. The trip ends with a farewell dinner.

Day by day

Itinerary

Day1Arrival in Marrakech

Welcome to Marrakech, the Red City. Look for the We Gravel Morocco sign at Menara Airport, and we'll drive you to your riad. Explore the city if you like, then meet the group and your guide to go through the trip.

Accommodation Riad or hotelMeals DinnerLevel Easy
Day2Marrakech (450 m) to Azib Tamsoult (2,400 m)6 hours

After breakfast we drive to Imlil and meet the muleteers. We walk up to the Tizi Mzik pass (2,450 m) and have lunch with a view over the Imnan valley. Then we continue to Azib Tamsoult and camp for the night.

Walking 6 hoursElevation +750 m / -350 mDrive 1.5 hoursAccommodation CampMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy to moderate
Day3Azib Tamsoult to the Aguelzim pass (3,550 m) and the Toubkal refuge (3,207 m)6.5 hours

A steady climb on an old mule path brings us to the Aguelzim pass, with a superb view of the Toubkal massif. After a picnic we go down to the refuge and camp for the night.

Walking 6.5 hoursElevation +1100 m / -300 mAccommodation Wild campMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day4Toubkal summit (4,167 m) and Aremd (1,900 m)9 hours

Summit day. We start early and reach the top in about three and a half hours. On a clear day you can see for miles in every direction. After some time on the summit we go back down to the refuge for lunch, then continue to Aremd for a night in a guesthouse.

Walking 9 hoursElevation +960 m / -2267 mAccommodation Guesthouse, Dar Aremd or similarMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate to hard
Day5Aremd to Imlil and Marrakech45 minutes

A short walk down the valley brings us back to Imlil, where the trek ends. We drive to Marrakech, and after lunch you have a guided tour of the city's mosques, palaces and gardens. Then you're free to explore until the farewell dinner.

Walking 45 minutesDrive 1.5 hoursAccommodation Riad or hotelMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
