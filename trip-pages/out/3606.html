The tour

Tour overview

M'Goun (4,071 m) is the second-highest mountain in North Africa, and much quieter than Toubkal. On this 8-day trek you walk up from the green Ait Bouguemez valley, cross passes above 3,400 m, follow old nomad paths and camp on high plateaus before the summit day.

The route is wild and varied. You see green fields, Berber villages, deep valleys and open high mountains. On the way in and out you also visit the natural bridge of Imi n'Ifri and the Ouzoud waterfalls.

Day by day

Itinerary

Day1Arrival in Marrakech

Your driver meets you at Menara Airport and takes you to your hotel. In the late afternoon your mountain guide briefs you on the trek, and in the evening you sit down to your first Moroccan dinner.

Accommodation Hotel or riadMeals DinnerLevel Easy
Day2Marrakech to the Ait Bouguemez valley via Imi n'Ifri2.5 hours

We leave the city behind and drive to the foothills of the central High Atlas. On the way we stop at Imi n'Ifri, a natural limestone bridge carved by the river that still flows under it. We have a picnic after a short walk across the bridge, then drive up into the hidden Ait Bouguemez valley. After mint tea with the muleteers, we walk up the Arous gorge to the pastures of Azib n'Ikiss (2,250 m) and camp for the night.

Walking 2.5 hoursElevation +450 mAccommodation CampMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day3Ikiss (2,250 m) to the Tarkdit plateau (2,900 m)5 hours

The bags go on the mules and we climb steadily for about three hours to the Aghouri pass (3,400 m), with our first view of the M'Goun summit. After a slow picnic we follow a nomad path down to the Tarkdit plateau and camp.

Walking 5 hoursElevation +1150 m / -500 mAccommodation CampMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day4M'Goun summit (4,071 m) to Oulilimt (2,600 m)9 hours

The big day. The climb isn't technical but the trail is steep, and it's a long, tough mountain day. From the top the view takes in the High Atlas, the Anti-Atlas and the Rose Valley. We go down to the Oulilimt valley to camp.

Note: If you'd rather skip the summit, you can walk with the muleteers to the next camp, an easier walk of about 5–6 hours.

Walking 9 hoursElevation +1171 m / -1471 mAccommodation CampMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate to hard
Day5Oulilimt (2,600 m) to Ain Aflafal (2,300 m)5 hours

We start by going down to Tighramt, below M'Goun, then walk an easy five hours through the wild Oulilimt valley to Ain Aflafal, with a picnic by the river on the way. Night in camp at Tighramt n'Ait Hemd.

Walking 5 hoursElevation +240 m / -300 mAccommodation CampMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day6Ain Aflafal (2,300 m) to Tabant (2,000 m)6.5 hours

We head back to the Happy Valley over the Tizi n'Ait Imi pass (2,900 m). The 550 m climb is rewarded with a great view over the M'Goun range. After a picnic at the pass, a 950 m descent brings us to Tabant, the main village of the valley and the end of the trek. Night in a guesthouse.

Walking 6.5 hoursElevation +600 m / -1050 mAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day7Tabant to Marrakech via Azilal and the Ouzoud waterfalls

We say goodbye to the trekking team and drive back to Marrakech via Azilal and the Ouzoud waterfalls. You reach your hotel in the early afternoon, with time to visit the medina and Jemaa el-Fna square before the farewell dinner.
