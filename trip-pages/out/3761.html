The tour

Tour overview

Can't choose between hiking and cycling? This 8-day tour gives you both. You spend two full days walking in the Ait Bouguemez, the 'Happy Valley', below M'Goun (4,071 m), the second-highest mountain in Morocco. Then you ride for three days through the M'Goun Geopark to Bin El Ouidane lake and the Ouzoud waterfalls.

The walking is easy, the riding is moderate, and the 4x4 is always close by. It's a good choice for couples and groups with mixed fitness.

Day by day

Itinerary

Day1Arrival in Marrakech

We meet you at Marrakech Menara Airport and drive you to your hotel, 15 minutes away. Later you meet your guide for a briefing about the tour. If you have time, visit the old town, Bahia Palace and Jemaa el-Fna square.

Drive 15 minutesAccommodation HotelLevel Easy
Day2Marrakech to the Ait Bouguemez Valley1 hour

A five-hour drive takes us into the mountains, with a stop at the natural bridge of Imi n'Ifri and lunch in the village of Ait Bouali. In the Happy Valley we meet the muleteers and walk for an hour to our guesthouse.

Walking 1 hourDrive 5 hoursAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day3Tizi n'Ait Imi and the Sidi Moussa granary5 hours

A 4–5 hour walk takes us through the heart of the valley. We climb up to Sidi Moussa, an old hilltop granary where families kept their grain in small rooms. In the afternoon we walk up the valley from village to village to our guesthouse in Ait Imi.

Walking 5 hoursAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day4Ait Imi to Zaouit Oulmzi5 hours

We walk through the village of Ibaqalliwn to see dinosaur footprints left in the rock millions of years ago. Then we continue to Adazen for a great view of the valley. After lunch we go down to Zaouit Oulmzi for the night.

Walking 5 hoursAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Easy
Day5Ait Bouguemez to Assamar60 km

Breakfast on the terrace comes with a view of M'Goun (4,071 m). Then we get on the bikes. The first 8 km are easy, through potato fields and apple orchards. Then comes the hardest climb of the day, up to the Tizi n'Tirghist pass, with a big view of the Atlas range. After lunch a more technical stretch takes us up and down to our camp at Assamar.

Cycling 60 kmElevation +650 m / -650 mDrive 30 minutesAccommodation Camp or hotelMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day6Assamar to Bin El Ouidane lake50 km

Today's ride winds up and down through a long, quiet thuya forest on good gravel. It's a demanding but very rewarding day. We finish at the lake with a glass of mint tea at our hotel.

Cycling 50 kmElevation +550 m / -650 mDrive 20 minutesAccommodation HotelMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day7Bin El Ouidane to Ouzoud and Marrakech50 km1 hour

After a short transfer to Azilal, the last ride is mostly downhill through oak forests and olive groves, with a few short climbs. We reach the Ouzoud waterfalls in the early afternoon and have lunch with a view of the falls. Then we drive back to Marrakech, where the medina is at its liveliest in the evening.

Cycling 50 kmWalking 1 hourElevation +450 m / -780 mDrive 2.5 hoursAccommodation HotelMeals Breakfast, lunchLevel Moderate
Day8Departure

Transfer to Menara Airport for your flight. Check-out is at 11:30. We hope to see you again soon – ma'a salama!
