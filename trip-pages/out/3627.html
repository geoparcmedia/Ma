The tour

Tour overview

Mountains first, then the sea. On this 7-day trip you trek for three days to the summit of Toubkal (4,167 m), the highest peak in North Africa. Then you spend two relaxed days in Essaouira, a laid-back fishing town on the Atlantic with a UNESCO-listed medina. The trip ends with a guided day in Marrakech.

It's a good way to earn your beach time. The climb is hard work, and the ocean breeze in Essaouira feels well deserved afterwards.

Day by day

Itinerary

Day1Arrival in Marrakech

Welcome to Marrakech, the Red City. Look for the We Gravel Morocco sign at Menara Airport, and we'll drive you to your hotel. Explore the city if you like before the welcome meeting, where you meet your guide and go through the trip.

Accommodation Hotel or riad
Day2Marrakech to Imlil (1,750 m) and the Toubkal refuge (3,207 m)5.5 hours

After breakfast we drive an hour and a half to Imlil. We meet the muleteers and climb steadily on a mule path to the shrine of Sidi Chamharouch (2,300 m), where we stop for a picnic. Then we continue to the Toubkal base camp (3,207 m). The walk takes about five and a half hours. You sleep in the refuge, or in a tent if you prefer, from May to the end of October.

Walking 5.5 hoursElevation +1457 mDrive 1.5 hoursAccommodation CampMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate
Day3Toubkal summit and Aremd9.5 hours

Summit day. We leave around 2:30 am to reach the top for the best light. It takes about three and a half hours from camp to the summit (4,167 m). After enjoying the view we go back down to camp for lunch, then continue to the village of Aremd for the night.

Walking 9.5 hoursElevation +960 m / -2267 mAccommodation GuesthouseMeals Breakfast, lunch, dinnerLevel Moderate to hard
Day4Aremd to Essaouira45 minutes

A short walk takes us back to Imlil, then we travel to Essaouira on the Atlantic coast. The town used to be called Mogador. Its walled 18th-century medina is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and has long attracted artists.

Walking 45 minutesElevation -150 mDrive 3 hoursAccommodation HotelMeals BreakfastLevel Easy
Day5Essaouira4 hours

A free day to enjoy Essaouira, with a guided walk included. Photograph the sea walls, a mix of Portuguese and Berber design, and browse the souks, woodworking shops and art galleries. At the harbour, stalls grill the fish that just came in. In the afternoon, walk on the beach or just relax.

Walking 4 hoursAccommodation HotelMeals BreakfastLevel Easy
Day6Essaouira to Marrakech3 hours

We drive back to Marrakech for a guided city tour. You'll see Jemaa el-Fna square, the 12th-century Koutoubia mosque and the 19th-century Bahia Palace, have a traditional lunch and walk through the souks. The rest of the afternoon is free.

Walking 3 hoursDrive 3 hoursAccommodation HotelMeals BreakfastLevel Easy

Transfer to the airport for your flight home.
