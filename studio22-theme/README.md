# Studio22 WordPress theme (studio22.qa)

One-page site for Studio22 (photo & video production, Qatar). English at `/`, Arabic at `/ar/`.

- `studio22/` – the theme. `studio22.zip` – the same, ready to upload (Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload, or extract into `wp-content/themes/`).
- Contact details and photos: Appearance > Customize > Studio22 (phone, WhatsApp, email, Instagram, location, hero photo, founder photo, 6 work photos). Empty photos are hidden; with no work photos the Work section links to Instagram.
- All texts (EN + AR) are in `studio22/inc/content.php`. Founder text comes from the old studio22.qa Team page.
- The booking form sends the details as a WhatsApp message (no mail server needed).
