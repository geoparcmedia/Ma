Best day and night survival games

99 Nights In The Forest

Explore the forest and collect food and materials by day, then build shelters and keep your campfire burning before night falls. Animals and enemies get stronger every night, and the goal is to last all 99.

Sandbox World Builder 3D

A calmer kind of survival: explore an open 3D world, gather wood, ore and fruit, build shops, craft weapons and fight monsters at your own pace.

Best zombie survival games

Dead Rails

A virus has turned the Wild West into a zombie wasteland. Ride an armored train, raid abandoned towns for fuel and gear, upgrade your weapons and fight your way to the last station.

Tiny Zomby The Barricade

Hold the barricade from your watchtower, plant mines and survive six stages of zombie waves. A good pick if you like defense games.

Zombie Shooter: Evil Outbreak

A 3D survival horror shooter where you clear five zombie-infested locations with six weapons.

Best sci-fi and action survival games

Project VOID

Survive waves of alien drones, collect Void Skulls and upgrade between rounds in this sci-fi roguelike.

Doomfront Iron Breach

A sci-fi first-person shooter: blast through five sectors, from bunkers to a spaceport, with five weapons.

Countercraft Nether Strike

A blocky 3D shooter where you fight mobs with six weapons and set off TNT chain reactions across five sectors.

How to choose a survival game

  • Like building and planning? Start with 99 Nights In The Forest or Sandbox World Builder 3D.
  • Want to fight zombies? Try Dead Rails, Tiny Zomby The Barricade or Zombie Shooter: Evil Outbreak.
  • Prefer fast action? Project VOID, Doomfront Iron Breach and Countercraft Nether Strike are all about combat.
  • Short on time? Wave-based games like Project VOID and Tiny Zomby are easy to play in short sessions.
